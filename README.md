## Quick Hosting Guide (Free, Instant Public Link)

### Method 1: Netlify Drop (Easiest - 30 seconds)
1. Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser.
2. Drag and drop this entire `wedding_invitation` folder into the Netlify box.
3. You will immediately get a live public website link (e.g. `https://khathija-safwan-wedding.netlify.app`).
4. Paste that link into your Instagram Bio or send to clients!

### Method 2: Local Preview on Your Mac
- Double-click `index.html` in Finder to view it directly in Chrome, Safari, or Brave.
- To test on your mobile phone on the same Wi-Fi, run:
  ```bash
  python3 -m http.server 8080
  ```
  and visit `http://<your-mac-ip>:8080` on your phone.
